Our Academic Training

Built for Ministry, Missions & Kingdom Impact

Global College of Missiology provides focused ministry training for men and women who desire to grow in biblical knowledge, Christian leadership, missions and practical Kingdom service.

The academic year consists of two semesters. Each semester provides 30 days of focused academic and ministry training.

Students pay the programme fee only once for the entire academic year. The annual fee covers both semesters.

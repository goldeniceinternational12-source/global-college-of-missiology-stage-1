About Global College of Missiology

Training Men and Women
for Kingdom Service.

Global College of Missiology is committed to biblical, theological, missiological and practical training that prepares people for effective Christian ministry and Kingdom service.

Who We Are

A College Built for Kingdom Assignment.

Global College of Missiology exists to help develop people who desire to serve God and contribute meaningfully to His Kingdom.

Through biblical, theological, missiological and practical training, students are equipped to grow in knowledge, character, leadership and ministry effectiveness.

Our emphasis is not only on learning, but also on preparation for practical Christian service, missions and ministry.

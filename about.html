About Us

A Mandate That Became A Movement.

Global College of Missiology (GCM) is a Christ-centered institution dedicated to training, equipping, and deploying men and women for ministry, missions, and global evangelization.

Established in 2000 under Liberation Outreach Ministry in Isiokpo, Rivers State, Nigeria, GCM was born from a divine mandate given to Apostle Kingsley Samuel to raise Kingdom Commandos for end-time missions.

For over 25 academic sessions, we have remained committed to developing ministers who are grounded in Scripture, strong in character, spiritually empowered, practically equipped, and passionate about the Great Commission.

With 500+ ministers and missionaries trained, our graduates are serving God across communities, mission fields, and nations, carrying the Gospel and advancing the Kingdom.

More Than Bible School

Training is not simply about gaining knowledge.

At GCM, we believe ministry training is more than acquiring knowledge or earning a certificate.

It is about discovering your assignment, developing your capacity, deepening your walk with God, and preparing for Kingdom impact.

Our training integrates biblical truth, spiritual formation, practical ministry, leadership, missions and evangelization.

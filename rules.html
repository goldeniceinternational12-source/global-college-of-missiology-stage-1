Institutional Standards

A Culture of Character & Excellence

The Global College of Missiology is committed to creating an environment where biblical truth, spiritual maturity, academic responsibility, discipline, and respect are upheld.

The Global College of Missiology (GCM) is committed to building God’s people for Global Missions and Evangelization through sound biblical teaching, spiritual formation, academic excellence, and ministerial training.


All students are expected to understand, respect, and observe the standards of conduct and responsibility outlined below throughout their period of training with the College.

GCM Rules

Rules Every Student Must Observe

These regulations provide the framework for responsible Christian conduct, academic discipline, respectful relationships, and effective ministerial training at GCM.

01

Christian Conduct

Students must maintain godly character, integrity, discipline, humility, and respect for others.

02

Attendance & Punctuality

Students are required to attend lectures, devotion, examinations, practical ministry, and other compulsory activities and must be punctual.

03

Academic Integrity

Cheating, plagiarism, examination malpractice, impersonation, and submission of another person's work are strictly prohibited.

04

Respect for Authority

Students must respect the College leadership, lecturers, staff, and fellow students and follow legitimate College instructions.

05

Appropriate Conduct

Fighting, bullying, harassment, abusive language, immoral conduct, fraud, theft, and other behaviour inconsistent with Christian values are prohibited.

06

Dress & Appearance

Students must maintain decent, modest, and appropriate appearance suitable for a Christian ministerial institution.

07

Online Learning

Students must participate responsibly in online classes, submit assignments on time, respect lecturers and fellow students, and avoid unauthorized sharing of GCM learning materials.

08

Use of College Resources

Students must handle College property, books, digital resources, and facilities responsibly.

09

Social Media

Students must not use the name, identity, materials, or platforms of GCM without authorization or publish content that deliberately misrepresents or brings the College into disrepute.

10

Discipline

Violation of these regulations may result in warning, counselling, suspension, withdrawal of privileges, or dismissal, depending on the seriousness of the offence.

11

Graduation Requirements

Students must satisfy the prescribed academic, attendance, practical ministry, project, and other requirements before graduation.

✦

Student Commitment

By enrolling with GCM, every student agrees to abide by these rules and regulations and to uphold the values, vision, and mission of the College.

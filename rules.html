Student Handbook

Rules & Regulations

Building a disciplined, respectful and Christ-centered academic community where every student is encouraged to grow in knowledge, character and service.

The rules and regulations of Global College of Missiology are established to maintain academic excellence, spiritual growth, discipline, respect and a healthy learning environment for every member of the college community.

College Standards

Rules Every Student Should Know

Students are expected to understand and respect the standards that guide life and learning at Global College of Missiology.

01

Respect and Good Conduct

Every student is expected to conduct themselves respectfully toward fellow students, lecturers, directors, staff and every member of the college community.

02

Academic Integrity

Students must complete academic work honestly. Plagiarism, impersonation, examination malpractice and other forms of academic dishonesty are not acceptable.

03

Attendance and Participation

Students are expected to participate actively in scheduled classes, lessons, assessments and other academic activities relevant to their programme.

04

Submission of Assignments

Assignments and other academic activities should be submitted according to the instructions and deadlines communicated by the college or lecturer.

05

Communication

Students should communicate with college administrators and lecturers respectfully and through the appropriate official channels.

06

Use of College Resources

College learning materials, digital resources, accounts and other facilities should be used responsibly and only for appropriate educational purposes.

07

Appropriate Online Behaviour

Students are expected to maintain respectful behaviour when participating in online classes, student groups, discussion platforms and other college-related digital spaces.

08

Personal Responsibility

Students are responsible for keeping their personal information, account credentials and academic activities secure and up to date.

09

Spiritual Discipline

Students are encouraged to cultivate personal devotion, prayer, biblical study, Christian character and a commitment to the mission of the college.

10

Misconduct

Behaviour that seriously disrupts the academic environment, harms others or violates established college standards may be subject to appropriate administrative action.

11

Compliance With College Policies

Students are expected to follow official policies, instructions and updates communicated by the leadership and administration of the college.

Student Commitment

Excellence, Discipline & Character

Becoming part of Global College of Missiology means joining a community committed to learning, spiritual development and preparation for effective Christian service.

Every student is encouraged to approach their studies with seriousness, integrity, humility and a willingness to grow.

  • Pursue academic excellence.
  • Demonstrate honesty and integrity.
  • Treat fellow students and staff with respect.
  • Participate responsibly in college activities.
  • Represent the values of the college positively.

Important Notice

College policies and procedures may be updated when necessary. Students are expected to pay attention to official announcements and follow the latest instructions issued by the administration.

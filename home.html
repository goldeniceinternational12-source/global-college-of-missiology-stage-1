About the School

Global College of Missiology

✦

Global College of Missiology provides sound biblical, theological, missiological and practical training for believers, ministers, missionaries, church leaders and everyone who desires to understand and participate effectively in the Great Commission.

Our vision goes beyond education. We seek to develop Kingdom-minded people who can become effective missionaries, evangelists, pastors, teachers, church planters, mission leaders and Kingdom workers.

What You Stand to Gain

  • Knowledge — understand God's Word.
  • Wisdom — apply it effectively.
  • Character — represent Christ faithfully.
  • Capacity — handle greater responsibility.
  • Experience — serve practically.
  • Refined Vision — see beyond your immediate environment.
  • Passion — reach people with the Gospel.
  • Courage — respond to God's call.
  • Mission — participate in the Great Commission.
  • Certificate Upon Completion — issued after your assessment test.

Your calling deserves preparation. Whether you are preparing for pastoral ministry, missions, evangelism, teaching, church leadership or another Kingdom service area, GCM provides an environment where you can be trained, equipped, developed and deployed.

Courses Outline

One-Year Study Programme

✦

The GCM programme runs for one full academic year. The academic year contains First Semester and Second Semester, with both semesters forming one complete programme.

First Semester

  1. Theology of Christian Ministry (1)
  2. The Concept of Leadership
  3. Missiology (1)
  4. Church Administration
  5. Kingdom Economics
  6. Marriage and Family Life

Second Semester

  1. Dynamism of Understanding the Bible
  2. Graciology
  3. Theology of Christian Ministry (2)
  4. Missiology (2)
  5. Theology of Children Ministry
  6. Marriage and Family Life (2)

✦ First Semester + Second Semester = One Full Academic Year ✦

Certificate issued upon successful completion of the programme and required assessment.

Tuition & Fees

Simple, Transparent Annual Fees

✦

Tuition is paid once for the complete academic year. The annual fee covers both First Semester and Second Semester. Students do not pay a separate fee when moving from First Semester to Second Semester.

🇳🇬

Students in Nigeria

$25 Full Academic Year

One-time payment covering the complete one-year programme.

First Semester

Part of the same annual programme.

Second Semester

Part of the same annual programme.

🌍

Students Outside Nigeria

$35 Full Academic Year

One-time payment covering the complete one-year programme.

First Semester

Part of the same annual programme.

Second Semester

Part of the same annual programme.

Important: The programme fee is paid once per academic year. The $25 fee for students in Nigeria and the $35 fee for students outside Nigeria each cover the complete one-year academic programme, including First Semester and Second Semester. There is no separate semester fee and no second tuition payment for the second semester.

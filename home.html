03

Spiritual Formation

Grow in prayer, spiritual discipline, faith, character, and sensitivity to the Holy Spirit.

04

Turn knowledge into ministry through practical exposure, service, evangelism, and real ministry responsibilities.

06

Character & Capacity

10

Global Perspective

Prepare to serve beyond geographical boundaries with a vision for reaching people, communities, and nations with the Gospel.

About the School

Global College of Missiology

✦

Welcome to Global College of Missiology, a school that provides sound biblical, theological, missiological, and practical training for believers, ministers, missionaries, church leaders, and everyone who desires to understand and participate effectively in the Great Commission.

Our vision goes beyond merely educating students. We are committed to developing Kingdom-minded people who can become effective missionaries, evangelists, pastors, teachers, church planters, mission leaders, and Kingdom workers.

What You Stand to Gain

  • 1. Knowledge — to understand God's Word.
  • 2. Wisdom — to apply it effectively.
  • 3. Character — to represent Christ faithfully.
  • 4. Capacity — to handle greater responsibility.
  • 5. Experience — to serve practically.
  • 6. Refined Vision — to see beyond your immediate environment.
  • 7. Passion — to reach people with the Gospel.
  • 8. Courage — to respond to God's call.
  • 9. Mission — to participate in the Great Commission.
  • 10. Certificate Upon Completion — issued after your assessment test.

Your calling deserves preparation. Whether you are preparing for pastoral ministry, missions, evangelism, teaching, church leadership, or another area of Kingdom service, G.C.M provides an environment where you can be trained, equipped, developed, and deployed.

Don't just answer the call, prepare for the assignment.

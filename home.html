About the School

Global College of Missiology

✦

Global College of Missiology provides sound biblical, theological, missiological and practical training for believers, ministers, missionaries, church leaders and everyone who desires to understand and participate effectively in the Great Commission.

Our vision goes beyond education. We seek to develop Kingdom-minded people who can become effective missionaries, evangelists, pastors, teachers, church planters, mission leaders and Kingdom workers.

What You Stand to Gain

  • Knowledge — understand God's Word.
  • Wisdom — apply it effectively.
  • Character — represent Christ faithfully.
  • Capacity — handle greater responsibility.
  • Experience — serve practically.
  • Refined Vision — see beyond your immediate environment.
  • Passion — reach people with the Gospel.
  • Courage — respond to God's call.
  • Mission — participate in the Great Commission.
  • Certificate Upon Completion — issued after your assessment test.

Your calling deserves preparation. Whether you are preparing for pastoral ministry, missions, evangelism, teaching, church leadership or another Kingdom service area, GCM provides an environment where you can be trained, equipped, developed and deployed.

Don't just answer the call, prepare for the assignment.

Tuition & Fees

Simple, Transparent Annual Fees

✦

Tuition is paid once for the academic year. The annual fee covers both First Semester and Second Semester, with each semester consisting of a focused 30-day training period.

🇳🇬

Students in Nigeria

$15 Annual Programme Fee

One-time payment covering both First Semester and Second Semester.

First Semester: 30 Days

Second Semester: 30 Days

Total Academic Training: 60 Days

🌍

Students Outside Nigeria

$25 Annual Programme Fee

One-time payment covering both First Semester and Second Semester.

First Semester: 30 Days

Second Semester: 30 Days

Total Academic Training: 60 Days

Important: The programme fee is paid once per academic year. There is no separate payment for each semester. The annual fee covers the complete 60-day academic training programme consisting of two 30-day semesters.

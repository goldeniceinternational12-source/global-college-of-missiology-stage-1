Biography

A Life Dedicated to Kingdom Service

The story, ministry and vision of the man whose burden for God's people gave birth to Global College of Missiology.

Apostle Kingsley Samuel is a Christian minister, Bible teacher, missionary, author, mentor and Kingdom strategist with a passion for God's Word and the advancement of His Kingdom.

He is the founder and General Overseer of Global Word Assembly (GWA), where his ministry has been devoted to teaching the Word of God, developing leaders, strengthening families, raising ministers and helping believers discover and fulfil their destinies.

His ministry is characterized by revelatory Bible teaching, grace, wisdom, spiritual development, leadership training, missions, evangelism and practical Christian living.

“The Church must not only preach the Gospel, but also prepare believers to become effective representatives of Christ in their generation.”

Founder of GCM

Building People Before Sending People

Apostle Kingsley Samuel is the founder and director of Global College of Missiology, a ministry-training institution focused on preparing people for global missions, evangelism, pastoral ministry, Christian leadership and Kingdom service.

The vision is centred on building God's people for global missions and evangelization and raising what he describes as “Kingdom Commandos” — believers who are prepared, equipped and ready to serve God's purposes.

The Vision

How The Vision Began

The vision was born from a burden for God's people to be adequately prepared, equipped and sent into the places where they are called to serve.

More Than A School

The vision was never simply academic. It is about raising people who understand the Word, discover their assignment, develop their gifts and serve the purposes of the Kingdom.

A Teacher of the Word

Truth That Produces Transformation

One of the defining features of Apostle Kingsley Samuel's ministry is his commitment to the systematic teaching of God's Word.

His teaching ministry emphasizes the believer's understanding of God's wisdom, grace, power, identity in Christ, spiritual inheritance, destiny, and responsibility in the Kingdom.

He seeks to communicate biblical truth in a manner that is both revelatory and practical, helping believers understand not only what the Bible says, but also how its truth should shape their lives and ministry.

Biography

A Life Dedicated to Kingdom Service

The story, ministry and vision of the man whose burden for properly equipped Kingdom workers gave birth to Global College of Missiology.

Apostle Kingsley Samuel

Apostle Kingsley Samuel is a Christian minister, Bible teacher, missionary, author, mentor, and Kingdom strategist with a passion for raising and equipping believers for effective Kingdom service, global missions, and evangelization.

He is the founder and General Overseer of Global Word Assembly (GWA), a Word-based, mission-minded ministry committed to building God's people for global missions and evangelization through the Word of Wisdom, Grace, and Power.

Through his ministry, Apostle Kingsley has devoted himself to teaching the Word of God, developing leaders, strengthening families, raising ministers, and helping believers discover and fulfil their God-given destinies.

His ministry is characterized by revelatory Bible teaching, grace, wisdom, spiritual development, leadership training, missions, evangelism, and practical Christian living.

The Church must not only preach the Gospel, but also prepare believers to become effective representatives of Christ in their generation.

Founder of GCM

Building People Before Sending People

Apostle Kingsley Samuel is also the Founder and Director of Global College of Missiology (GCM), a ministry-training institution established to prepare men and women for global missions, evangelism, pastoral ministry, Christian leadership, and other areas of Kingdom service.

The college exists under the vision of building God's people for global missions and evangelization and is committed to raising what Apostle Kingsley describes as Kingdom Commandos.

The Vision

How The Vision Began

The vision that gave birth to Global College of Missiology began with a burden for God's people to be adequately prepared, equipped and sent into the world.

More Than A School

The vision was never simply about creating an academic institution. It was about raising people who understand the Word of God, discover their assignments, develop their gifts, and become prepared to serve effectively in the Kingdom.

Building God's People for Global Missions and Evangelization
Our History

From A Burden To A Training Movement

What began as a burden in the heart of one minister gradually developed into a movement dedicated to preparing ministers and missionaries.

2000

The Beginning

The vision began to take a more definite form through a ministry known at the time as Libration Outreach Ministry.

EARLY YEARS

The Librationites

The early students became known as Librationites. Their training focused not only on academic knowledge but also on practical Kingdom service.

GROWTH

Expanding The Training

The training expanded to embrace biblical studies, theology, missions, leadership, church administration, ministerial ethics, marriage and family life, children's ministry and other areas of Christian ministry.

GLOBAL VISION

The Vision Becomes Global

The assignment was never intended to remain restricted to one congregation, one city or one nation. The burden was always connected to the Great Commission and the nations.

TODAY

Global College of Missiology

The vision continues through Global College of Missiology, training, equipping and preparing people for Kingdom assignment.

A Teacher of the Word

Truth That Produces Transformation

One of the defining features of Apostle Kingsley Samuel's ministry is his commitment to the systematic teaching of God's Word.

His teaching ministry emphasizes the believer's understanding of God's wisdom, grace, power, identity in Christ, spiritual inheritance, destiny, and responsibility in the Kingdom.

He seeks to communicate biblical truth in a manner that is both revelatory and practical, helping believers understand not only what the Bible says, but also how its truth should shape their lives and ministry.

What Happens After Admission?

After your admission is officially approved, you will proceed through KYC verification, course confirmation, fee payment, account creation, and enrollment. Each academic semester runs for 30 days. Nigerian students pay $15 per semester, while non-Nigerian students pay $25 per semester. The semester fee covers the complete semester and is not charged separately for individual courses. Once enrollment is completed, you will be able to continue into the student platform when it becomes available.

One-Time Full Academic Year Fee

The programme fee is paid once for the complete academic year. The academic year consists of 2 semesters, with each semester lasting 30 days, for a total programme duration of 60 days. Nigerian students pay $15 USD, while students outside Nigeria pay $25 USD. The fee is not charged separately for individual courses.

What Happens After Admission?

After your admission is officially approved, you will proceed through KYC verification, programme confirmation, fee payment, account creation, and enrollment. The academic year consists of 2 semesters, with each semester running for 30 days, making a total programme duration of 60 days. Nigerian students pay a one-time $15 USD fee for the complete academic year, while students outside Nigeria pay a one-time $25 USD fee for the complete academic year. The fee is not charged separately for individual courses or semesters. Once enrollment is completed, you will be able to continue into the student platform when it becomes available.

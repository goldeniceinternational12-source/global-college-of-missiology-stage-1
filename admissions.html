Global College of Missiology exists to build God's people for global missions and evangelization through the Word of wisdom, grace and power.

Admission into GCM is an opportunity to step into a focused training environment designed to develop biblical understanding, spiritual maturity, practical ministry capacity and Kingdom leadership.

Our academic programme is structured around two semesters, with each semester consisting of a focused 15-day training period.

Prospective Students

Who Is This Training For?

GCM is designed for men and women who desire deeper biblical understanding and preparation for effective Kingdom service.

01

Ministers & Pastors

Those already serving in Christian ministry who desire additional biblical, leadership and practical ministry training.

02

Aspiring Ministers

Men and women who sense a call to ministry and desire preparation before stepping into greater Kingdom responsibilities.

03

Missionaries & Evangelists

Believers with a burden for missions, evangelization and reaching communities and nations with the Gospel.

04

Kingdom Leaders

Christian leaders and workers who want to strengthen their biblical foundation, leadership capacity and Kingdom effectiveness.

Academic Expectations

Come Ready To Learn, Grow And Serve.

GCM training is intended to go beyond classroom information. Students are encouraged to approach the programme with seriousness, humility, discipline and a genuine desire for Kingdom service.

◆

Come prepared to engage deeply with the Word of God and the subjects being taught.

◆

Maintain discipline and respect for the institution, lecturers and fellow students.

◆

Participate actively in the academic and ministry-training environment.

◆

Approach the training as preparation for practical Kingdom assignment and service.

◆

Familiarize yourself with the official rules and regulations of the college.
